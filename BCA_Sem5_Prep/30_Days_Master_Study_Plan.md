## 5 Golden Rules for 80%+ BCA Exam Scoring:
1. **Always Draw Diagrams First:** In 5-mark and 10-mark questions, examiners award 40-50% of the marks immediately if the diagram (Android Architecture, Activity Lifecycle, V-Model, .NET Architecture) is neat, boxed, and accurately labelled.
2. **Use Tabular Comparisons:** Whenever asked to compare (e.g., DVM vs JVM, Explicit vs Implicit, Method Overloading vs Overriding, White-box vs Black-box), use neat comparative tables with 4–6 clear points.
3. **Include Minimal Working Code:** Writing 8–10 lines of clean, compilable Java/C#/Python syntax with comments demonstrates true mastery.
4. **Use Standard Terminology:** Use exact keywords (e.g., *Ahead-Of-Time compilation*, *Dalvik Executable*, *Intent Filters*, *Cyclomatic Complexity*, *Common Language Runtime*).
5. **Manage Time in the Exam Hall:**
   * First 15 minutes: Read paper and plan Section C questions.
   * Short questions (2 marks): 2-3 sentences each.
   * Medium questions (5 marks): 1 page (definition + diagram/table + short snippet).
   * Long questions (10/12 marks): 2.5–3 pages (detailed breakdown + full architecture diagram + code implementation).
