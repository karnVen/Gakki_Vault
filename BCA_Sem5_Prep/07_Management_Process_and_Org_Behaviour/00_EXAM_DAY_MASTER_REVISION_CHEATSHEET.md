## 2. Fast Cramming: The 6 Core Topics Demanded by the Professor

### Topic 1: Levels of Management
* **Top Level:** Strategic vision, long-range policies, CEO/MD. Needs **Conceptual Skills**.
* **Middle Level:** Departmental tactical programs, bridge between top & bottom, HODs. Needs **Human/Interpersonal Skills**.
* **Lower Level:** Direct supervision, day-to-day shop floor execution, Foremen. Needs **Technical Skills**.

### Topic 2: Management by Objectives (MBO)
* **Author:** Peter Drucker (1954).
* **Core Rule:** Goals set **jointly** by superior and subordinate, evaluated against actual results.
* **SMART:** Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
* **Cycle:** Org Goals $\to$ Cascade/Joint Setting $\to$ Action Plans $\to$ Periodic Monitoring $\to$ Evaluation $\to$ Feedback/Reward.

### Topic 3: Functions of Management (POSDC)
* **P - Planning:** Goal setting, premises, choosing alternative courses.
* **O - Organizing:** Structuring roles, grouping departments, defining authority.
* **S - Staffing:** Manpower planning, recruitment, selection, training, appraisal.
* **D - Directing:** Guiding through Supervision, Motivation, Leadership, Communication.
* **C - Controlling:** Measuring actual vs standard; Feedforward, Concurrent, Feedback.

### Topic 4: Organisation Structure
* **6 Dimensions:** Work Specialization, Departmentalization, Chain of Command, Span of Control, Centralization/Decentralization, Formalization.
* **Tall vs. Flat:** Tall has narrow span, many layers, slow comms; Flat has wide span, few layers, high empowerment.
* **Structures:**
  * *Line:* Simplest, military scalar chain.
  * *Line & Staff:* Line command + advisory specialists.
  * *Functional:* Grouped by specialty (Marketing, Finance, Ops).
  * *Divisional:* Self-contained units by Product, Geography, or Customer.
  * *Matrix:* Dual command (Functional Boss + Project Boss); violates Unity of Command.
* **Formal vs. Informal:** Deliberate/official vs Spontaneous/social (Grapevine).

### Topic 5: Planning
* **Process (8 Steps):** Scan Environment $\to$ Set SMART Objectives $\to$ Premises/Assumptions $\to$ Alternatives $\to$ Evaluate $\to$ Select Best $\to$ Derivative Plans & Budgets $\to$ Implement & Follow-up.
* **Plans:** Strategic (3-5+ yrs), Tactical (1-3 yrs), Operational (<1 yr); Standing (Policies, Procedures, Rules) vs Single-Use (Programs, Budgets).

### Topic 6: Decision Making
* **Rational Model:** Problem $\to$ Criteria $\to$ Weight $\to$ Alternatives $\to$ Evaluate $\to$ Select $\to$ Implement $\to$ Review.
* **Conditions:** Certainty (100% info), Risk (probabilities/EMV), Uncertainty (Maximax, Maximin, Savage regret).
* **Key Analytical Tools:** SWOT, Decision Tree, Cost-Benefit, Pareto 80/20, Brainstorming, Decision Matrix, PERT/CPM, Break-Even Analysis (BEP).
